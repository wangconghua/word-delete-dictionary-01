A make.fun entry for [The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2).

`main.py` writes the one word to erase from the dictionary forever to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> whatchamacallit

## Why this word

The judge brief ranks its criteria most important first, and this pick
answers each of them:

1. **A real word a reader recognises.** Everybody knows
   "whatchamacallit" — it is the word you reach for when you have
   forgotten the word.
2. **Funny at first sight.** Erase it and there is no word left for
   the word you forgot. The joke needs no caption or defence: the
   deletion defeats itself the moment you read it.
3. **The humour comes from the word itself.** Its sound, its literal
   meaning ("what you may call it"), and the gap its deletion leaves
   in the language — the one fallback name for unnamed things, gone.
4. **Fresh.** The field will crowd the obvious deletions — "moist",
   "irregardless", "literally", "synergy". Placeholder words are a
   family almost nobody thinks of first, yet the pick is instantly
   understood.
5. **Exact.** One word, spelled correctly, with nothing added around
   it.
